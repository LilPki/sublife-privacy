SubLife 隐私政策

Privacy Policy · 生效日期 Effective: 2026-11-11

中文 English

简单来说：SubLife 没有账号，没有自己的服务器，不收集、不上传、不出售你的任何数据。你记录的订阅只保存在你的设备上。只有在获取订阅图标、更新汇率时，App 会直接访问相应的公开服务。

1. 适用范围

本政策适用于 iOS 和 macOS 上的 SubLife 应用（以下简称"SubLife"或"本应用"），由 LilPki（以下简称"我们"）提供。

2. 我们不收集你的数据

SubLife 不需要注册或登录，也不运营任何服务器。我们无法看到、也不会收到你在 App 中记录的任何内容。本应用不包含广告、数据分析或崩溃统计等第三方工具，不进行任何形式的跟踪。

3. 保存在你设备上的数据

以下内容仅保存在你自己的设备上：

  • 你添加的订阅信息：名称、价格、币种、计费周期、分类、状态、首次扣费日、提醒设置、备注、官网地址和图标图片；
  • App 设置：语言、外观、基准货币、每月预算、提醒开关、汇率数据等。

删除 App 会同时删除这些数据。你可以随时在"设置 → 清空全部订阅"中删除全部记录。

4. 本应用会访问的网络服务

为了显示官方图标和最新汇率，SubLife 会直接从你的设备访问下列公开服务。这些请求不经过我们，也不包含你的价格、日期、备注等记录内容。和访问任何网站一样，对方可能会收到你的 IP 地址和设备的基本网络信息，并按其各自的隐私政策处理。

5. 通知

续费提醒使用系统的本地通知，由你的设备在本地安排和发送，不经过任何服务器。只有在你开启"续费提醒"时，App 才会请求通知权限，你也可以随时在系统设置中关闭。

6. 备份文件

"导出备份"会生成一个包含你订阅记录的 JSON 文件，保存位置由你自己选择（例如"文件"App 或 iCloud 云盘）。该文件之后的存储和分享由你自行管理。"导入备份"只读取你选择的文件。

7. 儿童隐私

SubLife 不面向儿童，也不收集任何人的个人信息，包括儿童。

8. 政策更新

如果本应用处理数据的方式发生变化，我们会更新本页面并修改上方的生效日期。重大变化会在 App 更新说明中告知。

Privacy Policy

Effective: November 11, 2026

In short: SubLife has no accounts and no servers of its own. We don't collect, upload, or sell any of your data. The subscriptions you record stay on your device. The app only contacts public services directly when it fetches subscription icons or exchange rates.

1. Scope

This policy applies to the SubLife app for iOS and macOS ("SubLife" or "the app"), provided by LilPki ("we").

2. We don't collect your data

SubLife requires no sign-up or login and does not operate any servers. We cannot see, and never receive, anything you record in the app. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs and does not track you.

3. Data stored on your device

The following is stored only on your own device:

  • Subscriptions you add: name, price, currency, billing cycle, category, status, first billing date, reminder setting, notes, website, and icon image;
  • App settings: language, appearance, base currency, monthly budget, reminder toggle, exchange-rate data, and similar preferences.

Deleting the app deletes this data. You can also remove all records at any time in Settings → Delete all subscriptions.

4. Network services the app contacts

To show official icons and current exchange rates, SubLife contacts the public services below directly from your device. These requests do not pass through us and never include your prices, dates, notes, or other records. As with visiting any website, these services may receive your IP address and basic network information and handle it under their own privacy policies.

ServiceWhat is sentWhen
Apple App Store lookup
itunes.apple.com		 The subscription name, or a known app identifier When "Auto-match icons" is on and you enter or save a subscription; or when you tap "Change icon" or "Fill in missing icons"
The service's official website A request for its home page and icon files (no personal information) Same as above; if you entered a website, that address is used

If you don't want the app to go online automatically, turn off "Auto-match icons" in Settings. The app will then connect only when you tap one of the buttons above. All tracking features work fully offline.

5. Notifications

Renewal reminders are local notifications scheduled and delivered by your device, with no server involved. The app asks for notification permission only when you turn on reminders, and you can turn them off at any time in system Settings.

6. Backup files

"Export backup" creates a JSON file of your subscriptions, saved wherever you choose (for example, the Files app or iCloud Drive). Storing and sharing that file is up to you. "Import backup" only reads the file you pick.

7. Children's privacy

SubLife is not directed at children and does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, we will update this page and the effective date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
