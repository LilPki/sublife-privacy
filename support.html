常见问题

我的数据保存在哪里？

默认只保存在你的设备上。SubLife 没有账号，也没有服务器。如果你在 Pro 中打开了"iCloud 同步"，记录会存进你自己的 iCloud 私有空间，开发者无法查看。没开同步时，删除 App 会同时删除这些记录。

换手机时怎么迁移数据？

开了 iCloud 同步的话，在新设备登录同一个 Apple 账户即可。也可以在旧设备的"设置 → 导出备份"保存一个备份文件，再在新设备的"设置 → 导入备份"中选择这个文件。

截图导入会上传我的截图吗？

不会。文字识别用的是系统自带的能力，全部在设备上完成，截图不会离开你的手机。在支持 Apple 智能的设备上，还会用系统的端侧模型帮忙整理，同样不联网。

SubLife Pro 包含什么？

免费版可以记录 8 项订阅，续费提醒和备份导出不受限制。Pro 是一次性购买，解锁不限数量、洞察、每月预算、双列和三列布局、桌面小组件、iCloud 同步和备用图标，并支持家人共享。

换了设备或重装后 Pro 不见了？

在"设置"里点"恢复购买"，并确认登录的是当初购买时的 Apple 账户。

我有兑换码，怎么用？

在"设置"里点"兑换码"并输入，或者在 App Store 里点头像，选择"兑换充值卡或代码"。

为什么有的订阅没有官方图标？

图标按订阅名称自动匹配。没匹配上时，可以在编辑页点"更换图标"自己挑选，或填写官网地址后重试。

收不到续费提醒？

请确认已在"设置"中打开"续费提醒"，并在系统"设置 → 通知 → SubLife"中允许通知。提醒在续费日前的上午 9 点发出。

外币金额准确吗？

默认使用内置的近似汇率。在"设置"中点"更新汇率"可以联网获取最新汇率。

FAQ

Where is my data stored?

On your device by default. SubLife has no accounts and no servers. If you turn on iCloud sync (Pro), your records are stored in your own private iCloud space, which the developer can't access. Without sync, deleting the app deletes your records.

How do I move to a new phone?

With iCloud sync on, just sign in to the same Apple Account on the new device. You can also go to Settings → Export backup on the old device, then Settings → Import backup on the new one and pick that file.

Does screenshot import upload my screenshots?

No. Text is recognized with built-in system features, entirely on your device. On devices that support Apple Intelligence, the on-device model helps organize the results, also without going online.

What's in SubLife Pro?

The free version holds 8 subscriptions, with renewal reminders and backups always included. Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks unlimited subscriptions, Insights, a monthly budget, grid layouts, widgets, iCloud sync and alternate icons. It supports Family Sharing.

Pro is missing after reinstalling or switching devices?

Tap "Restore purchases" in Settings, and make sure you're signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I use a redeem code?

Tap "Redeem code" in Settings and enter it, or in the App Store tap your profile picture and choose "Redeem Gift Card or Code".

Why don't some subscriptions have an official icon?

Icons are matched by name. If nothing matched, tap "Change icon" in the editor to pick one, or add the website and try again.

Not getting renewal reminders?

Make sure "Renewal reminders" is on in Settings, and that notifications are allowed under iOS Settings → Notifications → SubLife. Reminders arrive at 9 AM before each renewal.

Are foreign-currency amounts accurate?

Built-in approximate rates are used by default. Tap "Update exchange rates" in Settings to fetch current rates.
